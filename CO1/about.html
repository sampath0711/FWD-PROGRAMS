About

KL University Hyderabad, established in 1980, is a private deemed-to-be university located in Aziznagar, Moinabad Road, near the TS Police Academy in Hyderabad, Telangana. It is accredited with an A++ grade by NAAC and recognized by UGC, AICTE, and other regulatory bodies. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across engineering, management, computer applications, and sciences. It is particularly renowned for its B.Tech and MBA programs, with students placed in top companies such as CISCO, TATA, Amazon, Wipro, IBM, and Infosys
